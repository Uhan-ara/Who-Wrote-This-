# Who Wrote This?!

> A chaotic multiplayer storytelling game where every player only sees part of the story. Write it, pass it, draw it, guess it. If “Drawn Wrong/Misunderstood” moments had a whole album.

## 🎮 What is Who Wrote This?!

**Who Wrote This?!** is a chaotic multiplayer storytelling game inspired by telephone games, Mad Libs, and drawing/guessing games.

The catch?

**You never get to see the whole story while you're writing it.**

Each player receives only the previous player's contribution and has to continue the story from there.

What starts as:

> "A vampire..."

might eventually become:

> "A vampire wearing Crocs at the airport furiously juggling pineapples with a rat in his pocket."

Nobody knows exactly how it happened.

Until the end.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

### 1. Join a Room

Players join using:

- A room code
- A nickname
- No account required

The initial version is designed for **4–7 players**.

### 2. Build the Story

The story is constructed one fragment at a time.

For example:

```text
WHO
↓
WHEN
↓
WHERE
↓
DO WHAT
↓
WITH WHOM

Each player only sees the fragment passed to them.

They do not see the entire sentence.

3. Pass It On

After writing their contribution, the fragment is passed to another player.

The next player continues the story without knowing what came before.

And that's where the chaos begins.

4. Reveal the Story

Once all fragments are complete, everyone finally sees the full sentence.

And realizes what they have created.

5. Draw It

Everyone receives the completed sentence and tries to draw it.

The drawing phase includes basic tools such as:

Pen
Eraser
Colors
Clear canvas
6. Guess the Drawings

The drawings are shuffled.

Each player receives another player's drawing and tries to guess the original sentence.

7. The Telephone Album

At the end, the entire chain is revealed:

Story Fragment
      ↓
Next Fragment
      ↓
Completed Sentence
      ↓
Player Drawings
      ↓
Guesses

The goal is to preserve the entire chain of misunderstandings.

✨ The Core Rule

The most important rule of the game is:

You only know what was passed to you.

Players shouldn't be shown the entire hidden story while they are contributing.

The game is about discovering how a story changes when every person only has a tiny piece of the context.

📁 Project Goals

The goal isn't to build another generic drawing game.

Who Wrote This?! is about the entire chain of misunderstandings.

A complete game should let players see:

What everyone originally wrote.
How the sentence changed.
The final completed sentence.
How someone interpreted the sentence through their drawing.
How another player interpreted that drawing.
The entire chain afterward.

The Telephone Album is therefore a core part of the experience, rather than simply a results screen.

🚀 Running Locally

Clone the repository:

git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/who-wrote-this.git

Navigate into the project:

cd who-wrote-this

Install dependencies:

npm install

Start the development server:

npm run dev

Then open the local URL displayed in the terminal.
